# Next step after a teardown or a remake

Follow [offering one next step](next-step-offers.md). This route has two
moments for a next step; whichever comes first is the only offer in the
conversation. Offer nothing when neither condition holds.

## The teardown is delivered and the remake does not follow

After golden-path step 5 (teardown, shot list, and rebuild plan shown), the
user stops there: they only wanted the analysis, find the remake too much, or
end without choosing frames. When the reference is a talking-head clip (one
person speaking to the camera for most of its length), point to one of these:

- **Talking Avatar & AI Presenter Video.** One front-facing photo plus the
  rewritten narration becomes a talking-avatar video, without a frame for

own music (see [the six red lines](compliance.md)), so the remake carries no
music from the reference; the track is added in the user's editor.
