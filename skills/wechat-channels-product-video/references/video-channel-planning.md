# Video Channels product-display planning

The free planning stage turns a user's real product photo and confirmed product
information into one focused Video Channels display direction. Its aim is a
clear product-first visual story with a title and publishing copy that the user
can adapt for their own posting context.

## Gather only the facts that shape the display

Use the inspectable product photo as the visual source. Reuse details the user
has already provided:

- product name and intended viewer;
- one confirmed use, detail, or handling moment to show;
- required package front, label, logo, colour, material appearance, accessory,
  or visual no-go area; and
- product introduction, new-release, catalog, or Video Channels display
  context.

Visible product identity may guide the visual plan. Do not turn a visible image
into an unprovided specification or performance claim. Keep absent factual
details out of the title, publishing copy, and visual prompt.

## Make a compact Video Channels story

Use one user-selected angle and three connected beats:

| Beat | Purpose | Example direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product opening | Recognise the item immediately | Package or product appears cleanly with a calm title-safe area. |
| Detail or use moment | Make one product detail easy to notice | Texture, opening action, material handling, or a supplied use context. |
| Clean ending | Leave the product easy to recognise | Settle on a tidy product view with room for the user's final wording. |

Avoid stacking multiple benefits, scenes, products, or unverified comparison
claims into a five-second clip. Write a product-led title and publishing copy
from confirmed facts and the selected visual moment.

## Deliver the publishing materials

Before paid execution, draft these free materials for the user to revise:

1. **Video title:** a brief product or use hook based only on supplied facts.
2. **Publishing-copy rhythm:** product opening, one confirmed detail, and a
   concise product-introduction close.
3. **Discovery words:** product category and user-approved use words, kept
   natural for the intended viewer.

The generated visual should keep the product clear and recognisable through the
opening, detail, and ending beats.
