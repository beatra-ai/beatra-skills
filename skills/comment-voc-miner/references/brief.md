# Writing the brief

The brief is the deliverable. It is written from comments that were looked up or
pasted. It is never written from typical audience language.

## Four blocks

1. **What they are actually saying.** A short read of the repeating objections,
   purchase worries, praise that names a reason, and questions. Mark inference as
   inference. A count that was looked up carries its read time; a missing count is
   missing.
2. **Verbatim lines.** Keep the audience's wording. Do not polish a line into
   marketing copy while presenting it as theirs.
3. **FAQ answers and live-commerce replies.** Answer in their wording. Claims about
   the product stay the user's to supply — write around a missing fact rather than
   inventing one.
4. **Spoken hooks.** Short lines a host can say, each tied to a comment that
   prompted it.

One revision of the wording is free. Regenerating the brief from the same comments
is free. A new lookup is not.

## Provenance

- A figure or line read from a lookup is marked as looked up, with the date it was
  read.
- A figure or line the user supplied stays marked as supplied.
- A missing figure is stated as missing. It is never estimated, interpolated, or
  carried in from what similar posts usually look like.
- Why a comment performed well remains inference. Mark it as inference, give the
  evidence, and say what would confirm it.
