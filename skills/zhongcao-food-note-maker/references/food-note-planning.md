# Food-note planning

Build the creative brief before preparing a paid request. A strong REDnote food
note feels like one food or restaurant story, not unrelated food images.

## Build the food-note card

Capture only facts and decisions needed for the creator's story:

```text
Food anchor: source photo role or described dish / restaurant visit
Must-keeps: user-confirmed dish / ingredients / plating / tableware / packaging / named visual details
Visit setting: restaurant / café / dessert stop / home table / new-menu moment
Audience: personal followers / food community / restaurant audience
Story angle: food diary / dish highlight / café visit / restaurant recommendation / menu introduction
Visual direction: warm natural / editorial / moody evening / clean minimal / playful color
Palette and light: shared set direction
Title-safe placement: top / side / lower third
References in order: food first, then mood / table / composition guides
```

Reuse known context. A clear food photo or concrete dish or visit concept is
enough for the default. Ask one compact question only when a missing food anchor,
dining setting, or visual direction would materially change the result.

## Choose the story angle

| Angle | What the set makes clear | Caption direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Food diary | What made the meal or visit memorable | Start with the meal moment, then name a user-confirmed detail |
| Dish highlight | Why one dish takes center stage | Introduce the dish, show close detail, then share the user's stated recommendation point |
| Café visit | The drink, dessert, or setting that defines the stop | Lead with mood, then move through food and table atmosphere |
| Restaurant recommendation | The user's stated reason to share the visit | Keep restaurant and recommendation facts limited to user-provided details |
| Menu introduction | How a featured dish fits the proposed moment | Keep menu, price, availability, and location facts limited to user-provided details |

## Keep the sequence coherent

Carry three anchors through all slides:

- **Food anchor:** user-confirmed dish, plating, tableware, packaging, and
  named visual details remain central.
- **Visual anchor:** palette, light, and dining setting belong to the same
  family.
- **Narrative anchor:** each slide advances the same meal or visit rather than
  repeating the cover.

Use clean cover title space and minimal embedded display text. Prepare titles
and captions separately so the creator chooses final wording.

## Draft the free post package

1. Three title directions: mood-led, dish-detail, and audience-value.
2. A concise outline: opening hook, user-confirmed dish or visit detail, dining
   moment, a user-supplied recommendation point, and invitation to save or
   discuss.
3. A selective tag set: food style, venue or occasion, dish or ingredient only
   when supplied, and one natural REDnote discovery term.

The package names restaurant, menu, price, location, offer, brand, ingredient,
and taste facts only when the user confirmed them.
