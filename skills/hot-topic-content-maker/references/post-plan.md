# Building the post

The angle is chosen. This turns it into everything needed to publish.

## What a post is made of

Four pieces, always, plus the clip when one was included.

| Piece | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Cover wording | Earns the tap in a scrolling feed |
| Cover image | Carries that wording and the visual pull |
| Caption | Delivers the angle to whoever tapped |
| Hashtags | Puts the post where the topic is being followed |

## Cover wording

The cover carries the topic and the angle's hook in the fewest words that still make sense at thumbnail size.

Keep it to a headline of about ten characters or four words, plus an optional second line half that length. Longer wording does not render legibly at the size it is actually seen, and it will not survive image generation cleanly either.

Lead with the part that is surprising. A cover that leads with the topic and buries the angle looks like every other post on the same trend.

Offer two cover wordings and let the user pick. It costs nothing and it is the single highest-leverage line in the post.

## Caption

Structure it for the platform the user is posting to, but the shape is stable: open on the angle, deliver the substance, close with one ask.

- **Open** with the angle, not with the topic. The reader already knows the topic; the reason to keep reading is the take.
- **Deliver** the substance in short paragraphs. One idea each.
- **Close** with a single action. A question that invites a real answer usually outperforms a follow ask on a trend post, because comments are what keep a trending post circulating.

Keep it to what the platform actually shows before the fold, then let the rest run.

## Hashtags

Three groups, and the mix matters more than the count.

| Group | Count | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The topic's own tags | 1 to 2 | Where the conversation is already happening |
| Category tags | 2 to 3 | Where this account normally competes |
| Account or campaign tags | 1 | Continuity across posts |

Use the topic's real tag, spelled the way people are actually using it. An invented variant reaches nobody.

## The clip

When a clip is included, plan it as beats with on-screen and spoken kept apart, exactly as a video script would be.

| Field | Written for | Contains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On screen | The image call | Subject, framing, setting, lighting, motion — no dialogue |
| Spoken | The speech call | The exact words in speaking rhythm — no scene description |

Trend clips run short. Twelve seconds is the default and the ceiling is 15: the topic supplies the context, so the clip only has to deliver the angle.

Three beats is usually right — the hook that names the topic, the angle delivered, and the one ask. Budget the spoken fields at roughly four to five Chinese characters or two to three English words per second, and write to that rather than trimming after synthesis.

## Screen the copy while it is still free

**Superlatives and absolutes.** Words claiming top rank, totality, or permanence — best, first, number one, absolute, permanent, national-grade, 100% — are restricted in advertising copy in several markets, and their local-language equivalents carry the same restriction. Replace with the specific thing that is true.

**Efficacy claims.** Health, medical, curative, weight, whitening, and safety outcomes need substantiation the user actually holds. A trending peg does not relax this; it raises the audience.

**Prices.** Any price appears with its unit, size, or condition.

**Facts about the event.** Everything the post states about the real topic came from the user. Anything that did not gets cut rather than softened.

## Deliver it ready to paste

The user is publishing this within the hour. Hand over the caption as one block they can copy without editing, the hashtags on their own line in posting order, the cover wording beside the rendered cover so the two can be checked against each other, and the clip with its beats listed so they can re-cut it in their own editor if they want to.
