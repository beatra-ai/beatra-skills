# Writing the shortlist

The shortlist is the deliverable. It is written from creators that were looked up or
pasted. It is never written from typical creator stats.

## The memo

Write eight to twelve people. Fewer is fine when the user brought fewer. Do not pad
the list with invented names.

For each person:

1. **Who they are.** Handle, display name, platform, and the category they actually
   post. Mark inference as inference.
2. **Followers.** Only if a lookup returned the count or the user supplied it. A
   looked-up count carries its read time; a missing count is missing.
3. **Recent play median.** Compute it only from play or view counts that were looked
   up or supplied on the recent-posts page. Say how many posts went into the median.
   If those counts are missing, the median is missing.
4. **Interaction.** Likes, comments, or other counts that are present. Do not turn a
   missing comment count into an engagement rate.
5. **Content pillars.** Three to five recurring subjects from the posts in hand, not
   from what creators in this category usually post.
6. **Talk-or-not.** A call, with the budget band, market, and category fit as the
   reasons. This is inference. Mark it as inference and give the evidence.

One revision of the wording is free. Regenerating the memo from the same people is
free. A new lookup is not.

## Provenance

- A figure or line read from a lookup is marked as looked up, with the date it was
  read.
- A figure or line the user supplied stays marked as supplied.
- A missing figure is stated as missing. It is never estimated, interpolated, or
  carried in from what similar creators usually look like.
- Why a creator is a fit, or why their posts performed well, remains inference. Mark
  it as inference, give the evidence, and say what would confirm it.
