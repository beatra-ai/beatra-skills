# Local-business note planning

Build the creative brief before preparing a paid request. A strong REDnote local
business note makes a real place and visitor moment easy to understand, rather
than presenting unrelated marketing images.

## Build the business-note card

Capture only the facts and decisions needed for this visual story:

```text
Business anchor: accessible storefront / space / service / featured-product photo, or a concept brief
Business facts: user-provided name, category, city or area, and any details intended for the post
Must-keeps: user-confirmed sign, space, service, product, brand details, people, and named visual details
Audience: nearby visitors / regular customers / new local audiences / event guests
Visitor angle: new-store discovery / service highlight / local guide / event invitation / seasonal discovery
Visual direction: warm local / editorial / clean minimal / calm premium / playful color
Palette and light: shared set direction
Title-safe placement: top / side / lower third
References in order: business anchor first, then mood / composition / brand guides
```

Reuse known context. A business anchor or clear concept brief is enough for the
default. Ask one compact question only when the anchor, visitor angle, or visual
direction is missing.

## Choose the local-post angle

| Angle | What the set makes clear | Caption direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New-store discovery | What makes the place immediately recognisable | Begin with the place and a user-provided reason to notice it |
| Service highlight | What a visitor can see or experience | Introduce the approved service or offering, then connect it to the scene |
| Local guide | How the place fits one local outing or interest | Lead with the neighbourhood or occasion only when supplied |
| Event invitation | What the visitor will find at a named event or pop-up | Keep dates, times, venue details, and availability limited to supplied facts |
| Seasonal discovery | What the space, service, or offering feels like this season | Start with mood, then name a user-provided seasonal detail |

## Keep the sequence coherent

Carry three anchors through all slides:

- **Business anchor:** user-confirmed storefront, space, service, product,
  brand details, and named visual details remain central.
- **Visual anchor:** palette, light, and place language belong to the same
  family.
- **Narrative anchor:** each slide advances discovery from entry to experience
  to a visit-ready close rather than repeating the cover.

Use clean title space and minimal embedded display text. Prepare titles and
captions separately so the creator chooses final wording. Give every provided
fact a clear source in the brief before placing it in copy.

## Draft the free post package

1. Three title directions: place-led, experience-led, and audience-value-led.
2. A concise outline: opening hook, user-provided place or service detail,
   visitor moment, one supplied reason to save or share, and an invitation to
   discuss or visit.
3. A selective tag set: business category, local area or occasion only when
   supplied, and one natural REDnote discovery term.

The package uses names, locations, hours, prices, offers, booking details,
credentials, service outcomes, and availability only when the user confirmed
them.
